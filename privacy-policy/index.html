Privacy Policy

Super Mochi Princess - Sky Hop

Last updated: March 6, 2026

Hikarukensaya (HKS) ("we", "our", "us") built Super Mochi Princess - Sky Hop as a free, ad-supported game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed to be suitable for children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete that information.

Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Game progress (such as high scores) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any server.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob is configured for child-directed treatment, which means:

  • Ads are limited to age-appropriate content (maximum rating G)
  • Behavioral advertising and interest-based targeting are disabled
  • Only contextual ads are served

AdMob may collect certain device information (such as device type and operating system version) for the purpose of serving ads. This data is not used for tracking or building user profiles. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used by this app is Google AdMob for advertising. No analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs are included.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
